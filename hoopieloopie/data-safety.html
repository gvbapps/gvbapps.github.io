Hoopie Loopie — Data Safety & Handling

App: com.btech.hoopieloopie · Last updated: 7 August 2026
See also the full Privacy Policy

This is a plain-language summary of how Hoopie Loopie handles data — mirroring our Google Play Data safety and Apple App Privacy declarations. The developer runs no server and collects no personal data. The only data handling comes from Google's advertising SDK (AdMob) and, if you opt in, Google Play Games / Apple Game Center.

At a glance

  • No accounts No sign-up, login, or profile with us.
  • No developer server Game data (progress, coins, photos you add) stays on your device.
  • Kids not tracked Children (and anyone who doesn't confirm they're a grown-up) get child-directed, non-personalized ads with no advertising ID; only consenting adults may get personalized ads. We run no analytics, crash, attribution, or social SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing We do not sell or share personal information (CCPA/CPRA), and have not in the past 12 months.
  • Encrypted in transit All SDK network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS.

Data collected

Only the following, and only by third-party SDKs acting as service providers — never by a developer server:

Data typeCollected byPurposeShared
Device or other IDs

Google may process ordinary technical data (e.g., IP address as part of internet communication) on its own systems as an independent service provider, under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive, see, or store it.

Data we do NOT collect

Answered "No" on every store form for all of the following:

  • Location (precise or approximate)
  • Name, email, phone, or address
  • Contacts, messages, call logs, calendar
  • Photos & videos — any photo you add for a custom game or avatar stays in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded
  • Microphone / audio, files & documents
  • Health, financial, or payment data (purchases are handled entirely by Google Play / Apple)
  • Web browsing or search history
  • Crash logs / diagnostics (no crash-reporting SDK is used)

Children & families

Hoopie Loopie is a mixed-audience app for children and adults. A neutral one-time age screen gives children the full protections of a child-directed app — child-directed, non-personalized advertising with no advertising ID (COPPA / GDPR-K) — while only self-identified adults, with consent, may receive personalized ads. Every ad uses the strictest G / General content rating. There is no chat, no social features, and no user-generated content sharing.

Deletion & your control

  • We hold no developer-side data, so there is nothing on our side to delete.
  • All on-device data is erased by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • For data processed by Google (ads / optional Play Games), use Google's own controls: policies.google.com/privacy or your device's ad settings.
