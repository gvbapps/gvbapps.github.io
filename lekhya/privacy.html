Privacy Policy

Effective 13 August 2026 · Version 1.0 · Applies to Lekhya for Android and iOS

हिन्दी में पढ़ें

The short version: Lekhya collects nothing.

There is no account, no server and no analytics. The Android app ships without the INTERNET permission, and the app blocks network access at runtime on both platforms — so your financial information cannot leave your device, even by accident. This is not a promise about how we behave. It is a property of how the app is built, and you can verify it yourself.

1. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, transmit, sell, rent, share, broker or cross-sell any information about you or your money.

Specifically, Lekhya does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any other identifier
  • Your transactions, balances, budgets, goals, holdings or any financial figure
  • Your bank messages or payment notifications
  • Your location, contacts, photos or device identifiers
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers or behavioural data

There is no sign-up, no login and no profile. We have no database of users, because there is nothing for us to put in one. We could not identify you if we wanted to.

We will never use your information to market anything to you, and we will never send you promotional email — we do not have your address.

2. What stays on your device

Everything Lekhya knows lives in a private database on your phone, readable only by the app. That includes your transactions, categories, budgets, savings goals, accounts, investment and asset entries, shared-expense groups, bank-message templates, reminders and app settings.

You control all of it. From within the app you can export it as an encrypted .lkh backup or as a CSV file, restore it, edit it, or delete it. Uninstalling Lekhya removes it permanently.

3. How you can verify this

You do not have to take our word for any of the above.

  • Android: open Settings → Apps → Lekhya → Permissions. There is no internet or network permission listed, because the released app does not request one. An Android app without that permission is prevented by the operating system from opening any network connection.
  • Both platforms: the app installs a network block as the very first thing it does at startup. Any attempt by any part of the code to open a network connection throws an error instead of connecting. This is covered by an automated test that must pass before any release is built.
  • Offline test: turn on aeroplane mode. Lekhya works exactly the same, because it never needed a connection.

Lekhya asks for as little as possible, and every permission is optional except where noted. You can decline any of them and still use the app.

Lekhya does not request SMS, camera, photo library, microphone, location, contacts or calendar access. It cannot read your messages inbox — that permission is not in the app.

5. Third parties

Lekhya contains no advertising networks, no analytics services and no crash-reporting services. There is no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Crashlytics and no advertising SDK of any kind. Its fonts are bundled inside the app rather than downloaded, so even loading a screen contacts nobody.

There is exactly one exception, and it is not Lekhya reaching the internet:

Purchases. If you buy Lekhya Pro or leave a tip, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, in their app, under their privacy policy. Lekhya never sees your card, your billing address or your identity — it only receives a yes-or-no answer about whether the purchase succeeded, and stores that answer on your device. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy for how they handle payments.

Crash information, if the app ever fails, is written to a small local file on your device and is never transmitted. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage.

6. How your data is protected

  • Encrypted backups. A .lkh backup is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from a passphrase you choose, via PBKDF2 with 310,000 iterations. We cannot open your backup. Nobody can, without your passphrase.
  • Bank message text is encrypted at rest using a key held in the Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain — hardware-backed storage that other apps cannot read.
  • Card numbers are never stored in full. Any long run of digits is masked before it is saved, so at most the last four digits are kept. Lekhya never stores a CVV or a PIN, and never asks for one.
  • Excluded from cloud backup. The app's database is explicitly excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so your finances cannot leak through a phone migration.
  • App Lock can require your fingerprint, face or device PIN each time the app opens, and the app hides its contents in the task switcher.

7. Your rights

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you rights over personal data a company holds about you — to access it, correct it, have it erased, and to complain.

Lekhya is designed so those rights are satisfied by architecture rather than by a request form: we hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, erase or transfer. You exercise every one of these rights directly, in the app, without asking anyone's permission and without waiting:

  • Access — export everything as CSV or as a .lkh backup.
  • Correction — edit or delete any entry at any time.
  • Erasure — delete entries in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything.
  • Portability — the CSV export opens in any spreadsheet application.

If you believe we hold data about you, please write to us (§10) and we will respond. We expect the answer to be that we do not, and we would like the chance to prove it.

8. Children

Lekhya is a personal finance tool intended for adults and is listed for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and since it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.
