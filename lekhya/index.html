How it works

Your money diary writes itself — on your phone

A payment happens, Lekhya notices, and files it away neatly. Or you add it yourself in a couple of taps. Every step runs on your device.

  1. 1

    A payment happens

    UPI, card, netbanking, cash — however you paid. Your bank posts its usual alert, or you tell Lekhya yourself from the Lock Screen.

  2. 2

    Lekhya reads it locally

    On your device, Lekhya reads the alert into a transaction — merchant, amount and a suggested category. Nothing is uploaded.

  3. 3

    It's filed and understood

    It lands in your ledger, counts toward your budget, and feeds your trends, your net worth and your goals — all stored, encrypted, on your phone.

What's inside

Everything you need to stay on top of money

Considered, calm features — no gamification, no nagging, no dark patterns.

Spends file themselves

Reads the payment alerts your bank and payment apps already post — UPI, card or netbanking — and files each one in seconds. Or share a bank message into Lekhya, or copy it. All read on your phone, never your inbox.

Budgets that forecast

Set overall and per-category budgets with a live month-end projection, so you see where you'll land before you get there.

Clear insights

Spending by category, twelve months of spend-vs-income trends, and a tidy monthly summary — facts, never judgement.

Commitments & goals

Private by architecture

Not a privacy promise. A privacy design.

Most apps ask you to trust a policy. Lekhya removes the need to trust at all — the app has no internet permission, so your data cannot leave your phone, even if we wanted it to.

  • No INTERNET permission in the app — check it yourself in Settings → App info
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs (no Firebase, no Mixpanel)
  • No crash reporting that phones home
  • Lekhya never reads your inbox — it has no SMS permission at all
  • Backups are encrypted with your passphrase — only you hold the key
