How it works

Your spends record themselves, on your phone

A payment happens, Auto-record notices, and it's filed away neatly. Or you add it yourself in a couple of taps. Every step runs on your device.

  1. 1

    A payment happens

    UPI, card, wallet, netbanking, cash — however you paid. Your bank sends its usual alert, or you tell Lekhya yourself from the Lock Screen.

  2. 2

    Auto-record reads it, locally

    With your permission, Lekhya reads the financial transaction alerts that reach your phone (bank, card, UPI and wallet SMS, and payment app notifications) and turns each into a transaction. Never your inbox. Nothing is uploaded.

  3. 3

    It's filed and understood

    It lands in your ledger, counts toward your budget, and feeds your trends, your net worth and your goals — all stored on your phone.

What's inside today

Money is chapter one

Considered, calm features — no gamification, no nagging, no dark patterns.

Auto-record

Turn it on once and every bank, card, UPI and wallet alert gets recorded for you in seconds. Or share or copy a bank message, or import a CSV, PDF statement or screenshot. Only with your permission. Only on your phone.

Budgets that forecast

Set overall and per-category budgets with a live month-end projection, so you see where you'll land before you get there.

Clear insights

Spending by category, twelve months of spend-vs-income trends, and a tidy monthly summary — facts, never judgement.

Recurring detection

Spots EMIs, subscriptions, and rent automatically, and nudges you only when something's due soon.

Encrypted backups, on your schedule

AES-256 .lkh backups locked with your passphrase — any time, or automatically daily or weekly (Pro). On iPhone, to a folder you pick — on the phone or in your own iCloud Drive; on Android, to a folder on your phone. Only you can open them.

English & हिन्दी

Full Hindi and English, with Indian ₹ lakh and crore formatting that reads the way your bank writes it.

Everything you own, in one place

Track gold and silver, stocks, mutual funds and SIPs, fixed deposits and manual accounts — and see your net worth roll up across all of them. Every rate is one you enter, so nothing is ever fetched from anywhere.

Commitments & goals

EMIs, rent and subscriptions detected automatically or added by hand, with reminders before they're due. Savings goals you can link to a real holding.

Split with friends

Shared expenses, per-person balances and settle-up suggestions — without anyone needing an account, because there are no accounts.

One tap to add

Touch and hold the app icon, or add straight from your Lock Screen — a silent shortcut on Android, a Lock Screen widget on iPhone. No amount is ever shown on a locked screen.
