1. What Lekhya is

Lekhya is a personal finance app that runs entirely on your device. It records money you tell it about — by reading payment alerts on your phone, by parsing a message you share or copy, or by you typing it in — and helps you see and plan your spending.

Lekhya does not connect to your bank. It does not move money, hold money, or execute any transaction. It has no access to any account of yours. It reads notifications and messages that are already on your phone, and nothing more.

2. Lekhya is not financial advice

Everything Lekhya shows you — budgets, forecasts, insights, net worth, returns on the holdings you enter — is arithmetic performed on figures you supplied. It is information, not advice.

Lekhya does not recommend any investment, product, fund, stock, deposit or institution, and it never will. It has no price feed: every rate, NAV and current value in the app is one you typed in yourself, and it is only as current as you last made it. Before making a financial decision, please consult a qualified adviser.

3. Accuracy of automatic capture

Lekhya reads payment alerts using patterns that recognise common Indian bank and UPI message formats. Banks change their wording without notice, and some formats are ambiguous. So automatic capture is a convenience, not a guarantee:

  • A payment may be missed, captured twice, or filed with the wrong merchant, amount or category.
  • Every entry is editable, and you should treat Lekhya's figures as your own working record — not as a statement of account.
  • Your bank's own statement is the authority on what you actually spent. Where Lekhya and your bank disagree, your bank is right.

4. Backups and your passphrase

If you forget your backup passphrase, your backup is gone. Permanently.

A .lkh backup is encrypted with a key derived from the passphrase you choose. That passphrase is never stored anywhere — not in the app, not on your device, and certainly not with us. This is exactly why the file is safe to keep in cloud storage or email it to yourself.

It also means there is no reset link, no recovery question, and no support request that can open it. We cannot help you. Nobody can. Please write your passphrase down and keep it somewhere safe.

You are responsible for making backups. Uninstalling the app, wiping the device, or losing the phone will destroy your data if you have not exported it. Lekhya reminds you to back up, but it cannot do it for you — it has nowhere to put a copy.
