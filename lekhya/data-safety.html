Why "Financial Info" says not collected, when Lekhya is a finance app.

Your transactions and balances live in a private database on your phone. "Collected", in both stores' definitions, means transmitted off the device to the developer. Nothing is. Lekhya has no server to transmit to, and on Android no permission to transmit with.

The same applies to "Purchases": the store, not Lekhya, records what you bought.

Which permissions we ask for

Every one of these is optional, and all of them work entirely on your device. See the privacy policy for the details.

  • Notification access (Android) — read payment alerts to record a spend
  • Notifications — show your budget alerts and reminders
  • Biometrics / Face ID — App Lock
  • Files — only when you save or open a backup or CSV

Lekhya asks for no SMS, camera, photo, microphone, location, contacts or calendar access, and the Android release has no internet permission at all.

Independent verification

These are declarations, and declarations should be checkable. On Android you can inspect the permissions of the exact file we publish:

aapt2 dump permissions app-release.apk

There is no android.permission.INTERNET in the output. See the security page for the other three layers that back this up.
